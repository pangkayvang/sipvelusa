The Origin

A Ritual in Motion

Velusa began with a simple belief: the everyday deserves beautiful moments.

Inspired by the quiet ritual of preparing matcha and the individuality of gemstones, Velusa was created to bring beauty, intention, and a sense of discovery to the moments in between.

Our mobile matcha experience brings the ritual to you—where craftsmanship, thoughtful hospitality, and a little luxury come together wherever the moment takes us.

Every creation begins with carefully selected ceremonial matcha, then unfolds through thoughtfully layered flavors, velvety textures, and delicate finishing touches. From our signature Gems to our artisan Gem-Puffs™, each creation is designed to feel distinctive, beautiful, and worth savoring.

Velusa travels to mornings, markets, celebrations, weddings, and gatherings, transforming ordinary occasions into moments to pause, connect, and indulge.

No rush. No excess.
Just intentional craft, genuine hospitality, and a softer way to savor the moment.

The Mind Behind Velusa

The woman behind the ritual.

AK

Amara Kay

Founder & Matcha Artisan

Velusa was born from a love of the modern matcha ritual—the quiet pause, the velvety texture, and the intention behind every pour.

Amara created Velusa with a desire to make everyday moments feel a little more beautiful. Bringing ceremonial matcha and thoughtfully crafted Gems to Kansas City, she transforms gatherings, celebrations, and simple pauses into moments meant to be slowed down, savored, and remembered.

For Amara, Velusa is more than matcha. It is an invitation to pause, indulge in the moment, and discover a softer side of luxury.

The Matcha Ceremony

A ritual of clarity, beauty, and intention. At Velusa, matcha is more than preparation—it is a moment. A gentle pause where craft, beauty, and mindfulness come together, revealing the character within every Gem.

Each step is intentional. The soft sifting. The first meeting of matcha and warm water. The rhythmic whisking that awakens its vibrant character. The slow pour that reveals its color, aroma, and velvety texture.

Nothing is rushed. Every movement has a purpose, allowing the beauty of ceremonial matcha to unfold naturally.

Finished with thoughtful details—from Gem-Puffs™ to Velvet Crown™ shimmers—each creation becomes more than a drink. It becomes a moment to pause, savor, and remember.

A ritual unveiled in every cup.
