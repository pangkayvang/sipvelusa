The Origin

A Ritual in Motion

Velusa began with a simple truth: matcha is meant to be a ritual, not a rush. We created a mobile matcha bar that brings soft luxury into everyday spaces — a place where calm, craft, and intention meet.

Our approach is built on texture, balance, and patience.
Every whisk, pour, and layer is designed to create our signature velvety finish — a quiet moment of clarity in a world that moves too fast.

Velusa brings the beauty of a modern matcha ritual directly to your mornings, markets, and gatherings.
No shortcuts, no noise — just pure, ceremonial grace in motion.

What We Stand For
